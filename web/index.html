    Backtest of these exact rules (last ~60 trading days, 5-min candles)

    Simulated on the underlying (index, or for MCX the global-future proxy in rupees): enter on the next candle after a signal, exit at the stop-loss, at target 2, or 15 minutes before the close (15:20 on NSE). After target 1 the stop moves to entry. When the stop and target fall in the same candle, it assumes the stop hit first. The option-chain and news votes are not in the backtest (no history). "After costs" subtracts an estimated round-trip option cost in points of the underlying. The chart shows each instrument's cumulative result in % of its price, so contracts of very different sizes can be compared. Zinc and the Bullion Index have no free history and no backtest. Option time decay is not modelled, so option-buyer results would be worse.
